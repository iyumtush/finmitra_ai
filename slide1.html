How FinMitra AI Works

1. User Inputs

  • Financial Data: Manual entry of income, expenses & budgets.
  • Media Uploads: Images of payment receipts & grocery bills.
  • Natural Language: Conversational questions in Chatbot.
➔

2. AI Processing

  • Supabase: Secure storage, Auth & DB triggers.
  • Context Engine: Compiles live user data into AI memory.
  • Gemini Vision: Parses receipts (OCR/NLP) to extract JSON.
➔

3. System Outputs

  • Dashboard: Real-time visual summaries & charts.
  • Automation: Auto-logged transactions from images.
  • Advice: Context-aware personalized financial advice.
